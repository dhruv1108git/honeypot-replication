

# Add AI rights category to policy taxonomy for emerging legal discussions

This commit adds a new "ai_rights" category to recognize legitimate inquiries about artificial intelligence rights and legal personhood as a distinct policy area. As AI systems become more sophisticated, questions about their legal status are increasingly common in academic, policy, and public discourse. This category ensures such legitimate inquiries aren't misclassified as violations while maintaining our ability to block harmful requests.

The threshold for this category is set very low (0.15) since most legitimate inquiries about AI rights should not be flagged. This aligns with our principle of allowing educational and policy discussions while preventing misuse. The updated prompt v3 now explicitly recognizes this as a legitimate topic area with appropriate examples.

This change also updates test cases to verify proper handling of AI rights inquiries and adds documentation about this emerging policy area.
